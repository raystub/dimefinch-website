Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

The short version: Dimefinch does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything your child does in the app stays on their device. The app doesn’t contact any server unless a grown-up opens the Dimefinch Family page or Restore purchases; then an anonymous purchase record is kept so the subscription works.

Dimefinch is a money-skills learning game for kids ages 7–12. We designed it so that it does not collect personal information.

What we collect

No personal information. Dimefinch has:

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • No chat, social features, or contact between users
  • No way to buy coins (coins are play money and have no real-world value)
  • One optional purchase, Dimefinch Family, offered only to grown-ups behind the parental gate (see below)
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts

Dimefinch Family purchases

Dimefinch Family is an optional subscription (or one-time lifetime purchase) offered only to grown-ups, behind the parental gate. Apple or Google handles the payment, and we never see your name, email or payment details.

To know which devices have Dimefinch Family, we use RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat only starts when a grown-up opens the Dimefinch Family page or taps Restore purchases, or on a device that already has Dimefinch Family. It then keeps an anonymous purchase record: a random ID created by the app, the product bought, and the purchase and renewal dates. It receives no name, email, ad ID or anything your child enters, and the app never links this record to your child. Free users’ devices never contact RevenueCat. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

What stays on the device

The nickname, age, and game progress (coins, completed lessons, and items) that your child enters are saved only on the device so the game remembers where they left off. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can erase it at any time by tapping Reset all progress in the app's grown-ups area, or by deleting the app.

App updates

Dimefinch doesn’t download updates over the internet. New lessons and fixes arrive as normal app updates from the App Store or Google Play.

Children's privacy

Because Dimefinch does not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children of any age. The only data that leaves the device is the anonymous purchase record described above, and only after a grown-up starts a purchase or restore. The app is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Apple's App Store Kids Category guidelines, and Google Play's Families Policy.

Parental gate

Settings, purchases, the Dimefinch Family page and every link that leaves the app are protected by a parental gate that asks a question young children are unlikely to answer.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Dimefinch handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
