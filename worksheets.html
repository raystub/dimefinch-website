Money Safety

🛡️ Real or scam?

Cut out the messages. Read each one and vote: real or scam? Circle the warning signs.

Text message · unknown number
🎉 YOU WON a free phone! Click this link and type in your parent’s card number to pay for shipping.
RealScam
Class app · your teacher
The field trip form is in your take-home folder. Please bring it back signed by Friday.
RealScam
Game chat · “GameAdmin_Official”
Free game coins for you! Just tell me your password so I can add them to your account.
RealScam
Card in the mail · Grandma
Happy birthday! I’m proud of you. Love, Grandma.
RealScam
Email · “Account Team”
URGENT! Your account will be DELETED in 1 hour unless you log in here right now.
RealScam
Email · your public library
Reminder: your library book is due next Friday. You can renew it at the front desk.
RealScam

Rule for everyone: if a message asks for a password, a card number, or says “hurry,” stop and show a trusted grown-up.

2 · Goal cards (example answers)

Bike $120 at $10/week = 12 weeks · Video game $60 at $5 = 12 weeks · Soccer ball $24 at $3 = 8 weeks · Art set $45 at $5 = 9 weeks · Headphones $80 at $8 = 10 weeks · Book $15 at $3 = 5 weeks. If the division isn’t even, round up: you need one more week to finish.

4 · Needs and wants (suggested)

Needs: healthy food, winter coat, water, shoes that fit, a bed, medicine. Wants: video game, candy, light-up sneakers, movie ticket. Discuss: shoes are a need, but light-up shoes are a want.
