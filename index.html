How it works

Learn it. Play it. Live it.

Every lesson is short, every quiz explains the answer, and every coin earned becomes a real decision: spend it now, or save it and watch it grow?

📚

Bite-sized lessons

Quick, colorful cards explain one idea at a time, followed by a 3-question quiz that explains every answer.

🪙

A real economy

Coins go in a wallet or a savings bank that pays daily compound interest. Kids see why patience pays.

🎯

Goals worth saving for

Save toward something in the shop, or track real money for a real goal, like a new bike, with a picture and a countdown.

🔊

Read aloud

Every card, question and answer can be read in a warm, natural voice, so early readers can learn on their own.

🧹

Chores that count

Set up chores like making the bed. Kids tap “I did it!”, you approve, and they earn coins.

📰

Something new every day

A one-minute money fact daily, a short video every Friday, seasonal events, and 16 badges to collect.

For parents

Built to be safe from the ground up

We built Dimefinch so you never have to worry about what's happening on the screen. A grown-ups area (behind a parental gate) shows a weekly summary with a question to talk about together, and lets you approve chores, set a reminder, and adjust the age level anytime.

  • No ads, ever
  • Free to try. One optional Family Unlock, no subscription, and only behind the parental gate
  • Coins are play money and can’t be bought
  • No accounts, chat, or contact with strangers
  • No tracking or data collection. Progress stays on the device.
  • Profiles for up to 6 kids on one device, each with their own progress
  • A weekly report you can share with a co-parent
  • Parental gate on settings and purchases
  • New lessons added through free updates
