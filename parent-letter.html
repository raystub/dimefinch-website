Dimefinch

Dear families of ,

This , our class is learning about money: earning, saving, spending wisely and giving. Alongside our lessons, we’re using Dimefinch, a money-skills app for kids ages 7–12. I wanted to share how it works so you can keep the conversation going at home.

What your child will do

  • Short, colorful lessons followed by a 3-question quiz that explains every answer. Every card can be read aloud.
  • Earn play coins, then choose to spend them or save them in a pretend bank that pays interest.
  • Play money games about needs and wants, budgeting and investing.
  • A one-minute money fact each day and a short video every Friday.

Safe by design

  • No ads, ever
  • No accounts, chat or contact with strangers
  • No tracking: progress stays on the device
  • Coins are play money and can’t be bought
  • Grown-up settings sit behind a parent gate
  • The basics are free forever; an optional Dimefinch Family plan adds more, and you can cancel anytime

If you’d like to use it at home

  • Search “Dimefinch” on the App Store or Google Play. Every lesson for ages 7–10 is free.
  • Tap the 🔒 in the corner to open the grown-ups area. You’ll see a weekly summary of what your child learned, with a question to talk about together.
  • Set up chores (like making the bed); your child taps “I did it!” and you approve them for coins.
  • Siblings can each have their own profile on one device (up to 6).

Questions to ask at dinner

  • What’s something you want, and how long would it take to save for it?
  • Is that a need or a want? How can you tell?
  • If you had $10, how would you split it between saving, spending and giving?

Using the app at home is completely optional. Thank you for helping your child build habits that last a lifetime. Please reach out with any questions.

Warmly,
